\section*{Supplement Overview}

This supplement expands on the three contributions of the main paper.
Section~\ref{sec:supp-problem} gives the full formalization of the problem
definition, fixing the DAG input, the capacity parameters, and the three-stage
evaluation protocol. Section~\ref{sec:supp-formal} provides complete proofs of the
paper's three formal results---the spill-inevitability certificate
(Theorem~\ref{thm:spill-inev}), Belady-margin stability
(Proposition~\ref{prop:belady-margin}), and the overflow-area approximation
guarantee (Theorem~\ref{thm:area-extra})---and discusses the applicability
boundary of each. Section~\ref{sec:supp-solution} unfolds the design details
compressed in the method section, covering candidate-order generation, the
address-assignment procedure, and prefetch-window selection, together with an
end-to-end comparison against an external reference solution.
Section~\ref{sec:supp-cgo} supplies additional experimental evidence: the
separation of schedule order from victim rule, the applicability validation on a
synthetic distribution, the CP-SAT oracle optimality checks, and the controlled
clean/dirty ablation.
