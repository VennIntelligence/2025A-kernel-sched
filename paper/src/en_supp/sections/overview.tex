\section*{Supplement Overview}
This supplement gives auditable details for the paper's exact-to-heuristic evidence chain. Section~\ref{sec:supp-problem} specifies P1/P2/P3 evaluator semantics and static backed/generated classes. Section~\ref{sec:supp-formal} proves the traffic identity, fixed-order lower bound, and certificate, with a contiguous-address counterexample. Section~\ref{sec:supp-solution} uses a pedagogical graph and Conv\_Case0 for an end-to-end walkthrough while separating production, nonuniform repair studies, and the fixed-order oracle. Section~\ref{sec:supp-cgo} reports complete P1/P2/P3 results, component attribution, one capacity sweep, all 36 synthetic and eight oracle instances, artifact boundaries, and negative results.
