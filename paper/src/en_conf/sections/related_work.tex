\section{Related Work}\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Joint schedule--memory optimization.}
COSMA~\citep{li2023combined} jointly optimizes operator scheduling, allocation,
and tensor replacement with an ILP, so joint schedule--memory optimization is
established prior art and we claim no such first.  Our setting is narrower and
finer-grained: multi-cache micro-operation DAGs with the benchmark's static
input-backed and generated spill classes, a dependency-frontier production
heuristic, and a fixed-order continuous-offset certificate that connects the
heuristic to a machine-checked optimum.

\paragraph{Register pressure and rematerialization.}
Integrated instruction scheduling and register allocation
\citep{goodman1988integrated,shobaki2013preallocation,shobaki2022register}
likewise reshapes live ranges under a capacity constraint.
Checkmate~\citep{jain2019checkmate} computes offline rematerialization
schedules, and Dynamic Tensor Rematerialization~\citep{kirisame2020dtr} is its
online counterpart for dynamic models; both trade recomputation for memory.
Our benchmark forbids recomputation and instead demands explicit spill and
reload operations with concrete offsets for a static kernel DAG, which is why
packing validation is part of our certificate.

\paragraph{Caching and replacement.}
Belady's rule for fixed request streams~\citep{belady1966study} and weighted or
variable-size caching~\citep{coester2022blockaware} already treat nonuniform
sizes and retrieval costs, and clean-aware replacement is established in
compiler-managed memory~\citep{liberatore1999evaluation,guo2004belady}.  We
accordingly make no general weighted-caching claim.  The contribution is the
concrete combination: a topological request order that the optimizer itself
may change, multiple scratchpad caches, canonical one- versus two-transfer
spill costs, and a lower-bound-to-physical-packing bridge.  Spill-free
compilation~\citep{chatarasi2026spillfree} is complementary; our planner
targets instances where the evaluator still requires spill traffic.
